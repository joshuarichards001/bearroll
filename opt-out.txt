# Bear Roll opt-out list
#
# Blogs listed here are left out of Bear Roll. The collector skips their posts
# when it scrapes the discover page, and drops any of their posts it has
# already collected the next time it runs.
#
# To opt out, add your blog's domain on a line of its own and open a pull
# request, or email hello@bearroll.dev and I'll add it for you.
#
# One domain per line. A scheme and a leading "www." are ignored, so
# example.com, www.example.com and https://example.com/ all mean the same
# blog. Subdomains match exactly: example.bearblog.dev opts out that blog
# only, not every blog on bearblog.dev — so if your blog has moved to a
# custom domain, list the old bearblog.dev subdomain as well.
#
# Lines starting with # are comments.
